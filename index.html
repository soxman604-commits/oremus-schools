About us

A caring learning community

Oremus Schools Ltd is a combined school on Mapanza Street in Ndola, offering learning from Baby Class right through to Grade 12. We aim to give every child a safe, orderly and encouraging place to grow in knowledge, character and confidence.

Our hope is to walk with families through each stage of their child's education, from the very first day of school to the final national examinations.

  • One school from Baby Class to Grade 12
  • Places for internal pupils and external candidates
  • Located on Mapanza Street, Ndola

    Admissions

    How to apply

    1. Send an enquiry. Fill in the form and send it to us by WhatsApp or email, or visit the school.
    2. Visit the school. Come to Mapanza Street, Ndola, to see the school and speak to the office.
    3. Submit documents. The office will advise which documents are needed (for example, the pupil's birth certificate and previous school reports).
    4. Confirm the place. Complete registration with the office and get ready to start.

    External candidates: please contact us about registration for the ECZ Grade 7, 9 and 12 examinations.
